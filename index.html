What it does

Everything the family needs, in one calm place.

1

A shared care circle

Invite siblings, a spouse or a paid aide. Everyone sees the same up-to-date picture.

2

Medication log

Tap “given” and the whole family knows — who gave it and when. No more double doses or missed ones.

3

Appointments and notes

Who’s driving, what to bring, and what the doctor said — saved where everyone can read it.

4

Documents in one place

Insurance, ID copies, care plans and contacts — findable in seconds, not hours.

5

One-page emergency summary

Medications, conditions, doctors and contacts as a printable PDF for the ER or a new caregiver.

6

Daily handoff notes

A short note at the end of each visit, so the next person picks up where you left off.

Good to know

Is it available now?

Not yet — we are building it with input from families on the waitlist. Join to get early access and help shape it.

How much will it cost?

We’re planning a simple, fair price for the whole family — not per person. Early families will get a launch discount.

Is it medical advice?

No. Cozy Circle Family Care helps families organize and share information. It does not give medical advice — always follow your doctor and pharmacist.

Who is behind it?

Cozy Circle makes easy-to-use activities, planners and care organizers for families and older adults. This is our first app.
